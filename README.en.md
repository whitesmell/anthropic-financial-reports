For a first reading, start with the prospectus summary, risk factors, management's discussion and analysis, business overview, governance structure, and financial statements.

## Future reports

## Notes on the translation

- This is an independent project, unaffiliated with Anthropic or the SEC. The Chinese edition is not an official translation.
- The translation uses machine translation with focused manual corrections; it has not been verified line by line. Check figures, units, charts, and legal and accounting terms against the English original.
- The SEC filing and its amendments are authoritative. This collection is for education and research, not investment, legal, or financial advice.
- Images, trademarks, customer case studies, and other third-party materials may have separate rights. Public availability does not necessarily grant permission to redistribute them.

## Contribute

Corrections to translations, terminology, and source links are welcome. When reporting an issue, include the filing and version, PDF page or section, the English text, the current translation, and your proposed correction. For financial figures, include the currency, units, and reporting period.
